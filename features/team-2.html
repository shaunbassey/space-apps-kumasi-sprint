How Mars-Likeness Is Calculated

TRANSPARENT MATH
Mars-likeness (0–100) = 50% Coldness + 50% Dryness
Coldness = clamp((25 - t) / 45, 0, 1)  |  Dryness = clamp(1 - r / 5, 0, 1)
Units: t = temperature in °C at 2 m, r = rain in mm/day, s = sunlight in kWh/m²/day. Source: NASA POWER (2025).
